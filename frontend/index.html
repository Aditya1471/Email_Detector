Process

How PhishGuard works

From email submission to explainable verdict in under 25 milliseconds.

1. Submit Email

Paste email text or connect Gmail / Outlook via OAuth 2.0 for automatic inbox monitoring.

2. Extract Features

NLP analysis, URL pattern detection, sender domain heuristics, and urgency language identification.

3. ML Inference

Trained Random Forest model scores the email 0–100. No email bodies retained in database.

4. Explainable Report

Full indicator breakdown, risk charts, flagged links, and optional SMS alert for high-risk threats.
