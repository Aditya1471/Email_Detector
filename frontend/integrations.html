Privacy & Permission Scope Disclosures

✓ What PhishGuard Accesses

  • • Read-only access to message IDs and metadata headers in your Inbox.
  • • In-memory inspection of sender address, subject, and body signals.
  • • Metadata indexing (sender domain, timestamp, risk classification).

✖ What PhishGuard Never Does

  • • We never send, compose, or delete emails on your behalf.
  • • We never store raw email bodies or attachments in persistent storage.
  • • We never crawl or visit embedded hyperlinks automatically.
