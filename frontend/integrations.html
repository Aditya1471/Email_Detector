Privacy & Security Guarantees

PhishGuard operates exclusively as a defensive security service under strict read-only permissions:

  • Read-only scope (gmail.readonly): Used solely to inspect message headers and text for phishing analysis.
  • Zero Email Modification: PhishGuard cannot send, delete, move, or modify any emails in your mailbox.
  • No Attachment Execution: Attachments are never downloaded, opened, or executed.
  • No Active URL Crawling: URLs are analyzed heuristically without visiting destination servers.
  • Encrypted Tokens: Provider tokens are encrypted at rest using server-side Fernet encryption.
  • Data Minimization: Complete email bodies are not stored in database records. Only scan summaries and indicators are retained.
