What PhishGuard Checks

  • Sender Lookalikes: Detects homoglyphs, typosquatting domains, and mismatched display names.
  • Embedded Links: Inspects IP-address hostnames, redirectors, and credential harvest forms.
  • Social Engineering: Scans for artificial urgency, threats of account termination, and financial lures.

What We Do Not Do

  • • We do not automatically visit or crawl suspicious URLs.
  • • We do not execute attachments.
  • • We never store full email bodies.
